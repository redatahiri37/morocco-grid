About this tool

An open, transparent map of Morocco's electricity and digital infrastructure — built for researchers, developers and policy teams who need a single view of where power is produced, moved and consumed, and where the data-center pipeline is landing.

Every feature carries a source, and every layer but one also carries a per-feature source URL. Nothing here is proprietary. The exception is power generation, whose 42 features cite twelve different sources and need per-feature URLs — see Known data gaps.

Methodology

Data is hand-curated from Global Energy Monitor, ONEE, OpenStreetMap, Datacentermap.com and operator disclosures. Each GeoJSON feature includes a precision flag (exact or approximate), a source, and the commissioning_year or announcement year.

Transmission-line routing is indicative, traced from ONEE network maps and OSM — not survey-grade. Industrial demand figures are public-source estimates, not audited.

Inspired by Pawel Czyzak's Data Center Siting Tool (transparency-first) and enersite.app (multi-layer spatial analysis).

Known data gaps (v1.6)
  • No sub-60 kV distribution network.
  • Transmission lines are approximate corridors, not survey-grade.
  • Industrial demand is estimated from public disclosures.
  • Renewable-potential layer (solar/wind irradiation) not yet included.
  • No live data feeds — everything is static GeoJSON.

Methodology

Every layer on this map is a static GeoJSON under ./data/morocco/. Features carry a source and a source_url so provenance is inspectable from the tooltip, with one exception: the power-generation layer has source_url unpopulated, because its 42 features cite twelve different sources. The national-HV layer records coord_method and coord_confidence in place of precision. Both are listed under Limits.

    Limits

    • Transmission-line routing is indicative, not survey-grade.
    • Capacity factors are not applied — figures are nameplate.
    • Industrial demand is estimated from public disclosures.
    • Announced DC capacity is the headline, not energised capacity.
    • Power-generation features cite a named source but no source URL.
    • National-HV features use coord_method / coord_confidence rather than precision.
